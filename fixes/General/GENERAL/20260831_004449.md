## Basic Connection Setup

MeetingBar A50's One-Cable Solution simplifies deployment - a single Cat5e cable from the bar to the touch console provides power and network connectivity to the touch console, eliminating extra cables.

The touch console CTP25 can be mounted on the desk or wall, and it offers flexible connectivity options with direct connection to the bar or via IP.

## Installation Steps

1. **Mounting the MeetingBar A50**: The recommended installation height is 110 cm above the ground, and the reserved distance between A50 and TV should be greater than 1 cm.

2. **Pairing the Devices**: For optimal pairing, connect Yealink MeetingBar and then Yealink CTP Touch Panel. The Yealink CTP Touch Panel can be powered via PoE.

3. **Initial Setup**: During the initial setup, users will need to choose between Teams mode, Zoom mode, or Device mode (BYOD).

## Key Features

The CTP25 provides a centralized touchscreen interface for one-touch join, call controls, camera adjustments, recording, participant management, and more - minimizing the need for a laptop in the room.

Signing in on the touch console synchronizes the entire system, removing the need to sign in on the bar. Additionally, a single USB-C cable from the touch console to the PC enables 4K sharing, BYOD, and PD charging.

For detailed technical documentation, I recommend consulting the official Yealink MeetingBar A50 Quick Start Guide and CTP25 user manual for complete step-by-step instructions specific to your deployment environment.
