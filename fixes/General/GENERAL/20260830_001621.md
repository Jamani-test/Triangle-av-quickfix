## Basic Installation

The recommended installation height is 100 cm above the ground, with a distance of more than 1 cm between the A25 and TV.

### Mounting Options
The A25 features a One-Cable Solution that simplifies deployment, with a single Cat5e cable from the bar to the touch console providing power and network connectivity to the touch console.

The touch console CTP25 can be mounted on the desk or wall, and offers flexible connectivity options with direct connection to the bar or via IP.

### Network & Display Connection
A single USB-C cable from the touch console to the PC enables 4K sharing, BYOD, and PD charging.

## Initial Setup
After booting up Yealink MeetingBar for the first time or restoring it to factory settings, it will enter the startup wizard interface.

Ensure that the Yealink CTP Touch Panel is on the same LAN as Yealink MeetingBar.

## Detailed Documentation
For comprehensive setup instructions, I recommend consulting Yealink's official Quick Start Guide and User Guide available on their support website. These documents cover detailed wiring diagrams, mounting instructions, and platform-specific setup (Teams, Zoom, etc.).
