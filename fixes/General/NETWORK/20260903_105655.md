## Issue
Why is my Araknis AN-520-AP-I flashing amber light?

## Resolution
Based on the manufacturer documentation provided, here's what an **amber/orange flashing light** on your Araknis AP indicates:

**Blinking Orange = The AP is not connected to the internet**

This appears consistently across the LED operation tables in the documentation.

---

**Troubleshooting steps:**

1. **Verify network connectivity** — Check that the ethernet cable is properly connected to the LAN port
2. **Check upstream network** — Confirm your modem/router is online and passing traffic
3. **Restart the AP** — Hold the reset button for 1-9 seconds (LED will blink green) to restart
4. **Verify DHCP assignment** — Ensure the AP is receiving an IP address from your network
5. **Check OvrC status** — Log into OvrC (www.ovrc.com) to see if the device is registered and what status it reports

**Note:** Internet source searches didn't return specific AN-520-AP-I documentation to cross-verify, but the manufacturer documentation is clear on this LED state. If the issue persists after confirming network connectivity, escalate to Araknis support or access the local web interface to check detailed logs.
