## Issue
We have a network system with Araknis APs (4 indoor, 2 outdoor) going offline randomly. What could cause this and how do we troubleshoot it?

## Resolution
# Araknis AP Random Offline Issues — Troubleshooting Guide

## Most Likely Causes

**1. Power Supply Issues (Primary Suspect)**
- **Indoor APs (AN-820/AN-520):** Require adequate PoE supply via the 2.5 Gigabit PoE-IN port
- **Outdoor APs (AN-520-AP-O):** Require IEEE 802.3bt PoE++ (30W minimum) — this is critical
- **Flag:** Your PoE switch may lack sufficient total power budget for all 6 APs simultaneously
- Random restarts when load spikes are classic signs of insufficient power

**2. Network Connectivity Issues**
- DHCP server failures or IP address conflicts
- AP losing its assigned IP address before going offline
- LED indicator blinking indicates the AP isn't functioning correctly

**3. Cable & Hardware Faults**
- Damaged or corroded Ethernet cables (especially outdoor units exposed to weather)
- Loose or unseated connections
- Cable category too low for distance (use Cat5e minimum, Cat6 recommended)

---

## Troubleshooting Steps (Numbered Order)

1. **Verify PoE Power Budget**
   - Check your PoE switch specs — total available watts vs. combined AP draw
   - Test one AP at a time on different PoE ports
   - Consider adding a dedicated PoE injector for the outdoor APs

2. **Inspect All Cables**
   - Check for physical damage, corrosion (outdoor APs especially)
   - Verify all Ethernet connections are fully seated
   - Replace suspect cables

3. **Network Configuration Check**
   - Use a network scanner (e.g., Fing — look for manufacturer "SnapAV") to locate all APs
   - Log into OvrC at www.ovrc.com and check device list for IP address assignments
   - Verify your DHCP server is functioning and assigning addresses correctly
   - Check for IP conflicts

4. **Outdoor AP-Specific (AN-520-AP-O)**
   - Verify mounting orientation: **ports must face downward** to prevent water collection
   - Confirm cable routing and entrance (bottom or rear) is correct
   - Check IP55 housing for physical damage

5. **Firmware & Reset**
   - Update to latest firmware via OvrC or local interface
   - Factory reset if issues persist (see manual for procedure)

6. **Access the AP for Diagnostics**
   - Via OvrC webconnect feature
   - Via DHCP-assigned IP address (check router or Fing)
   - Via default IP: **192.168.20.253** (requires direct Ethernet connection)

## Escalation
If offline issues continue after these steps, contact Araknis technical support with:
- LED indicator behavior logs
- Failure timestamps and frequency pattern
- PoE switch model and power budget details
- Cable lengths and specifications
