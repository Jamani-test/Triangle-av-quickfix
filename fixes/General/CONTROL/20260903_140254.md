How do I power cycle a Control4 Halo remote?

## Resolution
# Power Cycling a Control4 Halo Remote

Based on the sources provided, here's the procedure:

1. **Hold the power button** at the top of the device until the display lights up.

2. **Release the power button** but immediately continue holding the **back button for ten seconds**.

3. **Release the back button** after ten seconds. The remote will restart.

After the restart completes, the Halo remote may display the First Time User Experience screen with a QR code.

---

**Note:** The manufacturer documentation provided covers Control4 CORE controllers and IO Extenders, but does not include Halo remote power cycle procedures. The steps above come from internet source confirmation and represent the correct procedure for restarting a Halo remote.
