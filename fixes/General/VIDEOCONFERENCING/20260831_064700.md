# Yealink MeetingBar Options

Based on Triangle's documentation, Yealink offers the following MeetingBar models:

## Models by Room Size

**Small Huddle Rooms (2–5 people):**
- **MeetingBar A10** – Entry-level option
- **MeetingBar A25** – Enhanced features for focus and huddle rooms

**Medium Meeting Rooms:**
- **MeetingBar A30** – Integrated cameras, microphones, and speakers
- **MeetingBar A40** – Dual 48MP cameras, 8-microphone array, stereo speakers

**Large Boardrooms:**
- **MeetingBar A50** – Premium all-in-one system with triple 50MP camera array, 10m voice pickup, AI-enhanced audio/video

## Key Specifications

- **A25, A40, A50** run Android 13 and support Microsoft Device Ecosystem Platform (MDEP) for Teams compatibility and long-term support
- **A10 and A25** include mounting brackets, power adapters (48V), and CAT5e networking cables
- **A40 and A30** include HDMI 2.0 cables and expanded accessory packages

## Upgrade Note

The MeetingBar A20 has been discontinued and replaced by the **A40**, which is the recommended upgrade path.

All models are currently within Triangle Limited's video conferencing support range.
