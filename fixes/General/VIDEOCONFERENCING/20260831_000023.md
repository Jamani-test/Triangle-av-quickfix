## Issue
How do I set up a Yealink A50 headset?

## Resolution
There is a **product clarification needed**: Yealink does not manufacture an "A50 headset."

**What exists:**

The **Yealink MeetingBar A50** is a video conferencing system (not a headset) for medium to large conference rooms. According to the documentation, basic connection involves:

1. Connect displays via HDMI 1 and HDMI 2 ports (HDMI 1.4 or higher required, 1080p minimum resolution)
2. Apply the magnetic ring to the HDMI cable to prevent electromagnetic interference
3. Optionally connect the MeetingDisplay, MCore4, or MTouch Plus (purchased separately)

**If you need a Yealink wireless microphone solution**, Triangle supports:
- **VCM36-W** (wireless microphone array) — pairs via USB-C cable or Yealink RoomConnect software
- **VCM35** (wired microphone array) — 5m voice pickup range with acoustic echo cancellation
