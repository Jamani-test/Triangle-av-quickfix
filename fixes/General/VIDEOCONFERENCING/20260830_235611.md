# Yealink A50 Setup Instructions

Based on the available documentation, here's how to set up the Yealink MeetingBar A50:

## Physical Installation

1. Mount the unit at **110 cm above ground level** (wall or table-top stand)
2. Maintain **at least 1 cm clearance** between the A50 and your display

## Display Connections

**Single Monitor:**
1. Connect your display to **HDMI 1 port**
2. Ensure display supports **HDMI 1.4 or higher** with **1080p minimum resolution**
3. Connect the power adapter to the A50

**Dual Monitor:**
1. Connect both displays to the **dual HDMI OUT ports**
2. Secure all HDMI cables
3. Power on both displays
4. Navigate to **Settings > More > Device Settings > Display**
5. Enable **Dual Monitor Mode**

## Optional Touch Panel Setup

1. Connect the Yealink CTP Touch Panel to the MeetingBar
2. Ensure both devices are on the **same LAN or Wi-Fi network**
3. The touch panel can be powered via PoE (Power over Ethernet)
