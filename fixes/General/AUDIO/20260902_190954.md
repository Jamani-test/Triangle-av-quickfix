# PowerNode Edge N330 Setup

Based on available documentation, here are the setup steps:

## Power & Network Connection
1. Connect the included power cable to the PowerNode Edge N330 and plug into a working wall outlet
   - **Important**: Use only the included power cable to avoid warranty issues
2. Choose your network connection:
   - **Wired**: Connect an Ethernet cable from your router's LAN port to the PowerNode's Ethernet port, OR
   - **Wireless**: Connect via Wi-Fi (supports 802.11a/b/g/n/ac/ax on 2.4/5 GHz bands)

## App Setup
3. Download and install the **BluOS Controller app** on your mobile device
4. Launch the app and follow on-screen instructions to:
   - Add the PowerNode N330 to your network
   - Name your player
   - Complete Wi-Fi configuration (if using wireless)

## Audio Setup
5. Connect your loudspeakers to complete your two-channel system

The PowerNode Edge N330 supports multiple input options including HDMI eARC, Bluetooth (aptX HD), optical digital, 3.5mm auxiliary, and USB for flexible integration.

**Note**: The complete owner's manual for the N330 model isn't currently available in our documentation. For detailed technical specifications or troubleshooting beyond these steps, consult the Quick Setup Guide included with your unit or visit support.bluesound.com.
