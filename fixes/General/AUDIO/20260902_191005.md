# Power Node Edge N330 Setup

Based on our documentation, here's the setup procedure:

## Power Connection
1. Select the appropriate AC power cord for your region
2. Connect firmly to the Power Node's AC Mains input socket
3. Connect to wall outlet

**Specifications:** 100-240V 50/60Hz auto-switching supply

## Network Connection

You have two options:

**Option 1 - Wired (Ethernet):**
1. Connect Ethernet cable from your router's LAN port to the Powernode's 10/100 Ethernet port
2. Device will connect automatically

**Option 2 - Wireless (Wi-Fi):**
1. Navigate to settings menu on device interface
2. Select "Wi-Fi Setup"
3. Enter your network SSID and password
4. Follow on-screen prompts to complete

**Specifications:** Connects to 802.11a/b/g/n/ac/ax 2.4/5 GHz broadcast-capable routers

## Control & Operation

1. Download the Bluesound mobile app to your device
2. Follow app prompts to connect to your Power Node
3. Control your system from the app
