## Issue
My Sonos speakers are not playing music. How do I troubleshoot this?

## Resolution
# Sonos Troubleshooting: Speakers Not Playing Music

Follow these steps in order:

## 1. Basic Checks
- **Volume & Mute**: In the Sonos app, confirm volume is not at zero and the speaker is not muted. Check physical volume controls on the speaker itself.
- **WiFi Connection**: Verify WiFi stability and signal strength. Restart the Sonos app and speaker to refresh connections.
- **Music Source**: Test a different music service or local library to isolate whether the issue is source-specific.

## 2. Soft Reboot Speaker
1. Unplug the power cable
2. Wait 10 seconds
3. Plug back in
4. Wait for the status light to stabilize (if applicable, wait for solid white)
5. Try playing music again via the app

## 3. Full System Reboot
1. Unplug all speakers
2. Reboot your router
3. Plug in and reconnect speakers one by one

## 4. App & Firmware Updates
Update both the Sonos app and all speaker firmware to the latest versions.

## 5. Reset Network Settings (if issues persist)
- Reset network settings on the speaker
- Reconnect to WiFi
- If using a streaming service, remove it from the Sonos app, close the app completely, then reopen and re-add the service

If these steps don't resolve the issue, contact Sonos support at support.sonos.com.
